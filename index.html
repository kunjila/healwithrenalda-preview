Meet Renalda

It began at home.

My daughter changed the direction of my life. As a mother of an autistic child, I spent years looking for a way to make our days more supported and less overwhelming.

We live in rural Lithuania, close to seasonal, organic food and a slower rhythm. That helped. I began to practice what I was learning — first with her, then with myself — and I kept notes on what a real household could actually hold.

Immediate family began to practice. They saw changes too. Then friends. Then the families of friends. The messages became more than one woman can answer well.

The Library grew from those conversations: the sources I study, the order I use, and the things I used to say only at the kitchen table.
